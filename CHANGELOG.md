### Create: Central Kitchen 2.0.0
Create: Central Kitchen has been overhauled for the 2.0.0 version.

Farming Automation part is now a standalone mod: Create: Integrated Farming.

Ingredient/Content Integration part will also be split out to another standalone mod.

Create: Central Kitchen now focus on Cooking Automation only, for currently available features, 
see the **Features** section below.

#### Features
- Packager unpacking support for Cooking Pot (Farmer's Delight) and Keg (Brewin' and Chewin').
- Mechanical Arm interaction support for Cooking Pot, Skillet, Stove and Cutting Board.
  + Mechanical Arm insertion for Cooking Pot is for container slot only, 
  as the ingredient slots is properly handled by Packager unpacking.
- Add Heat Source support for all Boiler Heaters.
- Active Boiler Heaters now speeds up Cooking Pot and Skillet cooking.
- Automatically transform Cutting Board recipes (Farmer's Delight) that uses knife into Sawing recipes.
- Automatically transform Cutting Board recipes (Farmer's Delight) into Deploying recipes.
- Automatically transform Keg Pouring recipes (Brewin' and Chewin') into Filling and Draining recipes.
- Add Tree Bark (or Straw for Bamboo-like blocks) to runtime generated Sawing recipes for stripping wood/logs.

#### Future Plans
- Multiblock Keg with Brewin' and Chewin' integration.
- Multiblock Oven.
